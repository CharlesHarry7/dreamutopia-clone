Frequently Asked Questions

Credits, payments, and how generations are billed.

How do credits work?
Credits are a universal currency for both video and image generation. Each model costs a different amount of credits. Buy credits once and use them on any model.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cryptocurrency and Telegram Stars. If you pay with Telegram Stars you can buy them during checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a card.
Do credits expire?
No, your credits never expire. Use them at your own pace.
What happens if my generation fails?
Failed generations are automatically refunded. You'll only be charged credits for successful generations.
