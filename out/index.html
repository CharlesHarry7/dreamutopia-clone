Frequently Asked Questions

Is DreamUtopia really free to use?
Yes! Every device gets 2 free Lite video generations with no signup. Create an account for 10 credits (Lite 3 · Medium 5 · Pro 16 · Image Lite 1 · Pro 2). Extra packs are sold on Pricing via Stripe when configured.
What AI models are available?
We offer three premium AI models — Lite, Medium, and Pro — each tuned for different motion styles, quality levels, and generation speeds.
How long does video generation take?
Most videos are generated in 30–90 seconds depending on the model and complexity. You can watch live progress as your video renders.
What image formats can I upload?
We accept JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and TIFF files up to 20 MB. For best results, use a clear, high-resolution image. You can also paste a public https image URL.
Do I own the videos I create?
Absolutely. You retain full ownership and copyright of every video you generate on DreamUtopia — use them however you like.
Is my content private and secure?
Yes. We use encrypted transfers and do not retain your content after processing. Your uploads and generated videos remain private and under your control unless you opt in to the public gallery.
Can I use DreamUtopia on mobile?
Yes. DreamUtopia works in the browser on desktop, tablet, or phone. Add it to your home screen as an app (PWA). There is no separate App Store or Google Play listing.
What happens after my 2 free tries?
After 2 free Lite videos, create a free account for 10 credits. Credit packs are listed on Pricing (Starter $5 / 10 credits through Premium $200 / 500). First purchase adds 31 bonus credits. Credits never expire.
