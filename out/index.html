Frequently Asked Questions

Is DreamUtopia really free to use?
Yes! Every device gets 2 free video generations with no signup, no login, and no credit card required. Just upload an image and start creating.
What AI models are available?
We offer three premium AI models — Lite, Medium, and Pro — each tuned for different motion styles, quality levels, and generation speeds.
How long does video generation take?
Most videos are generated in 30–90 seconds depending on the model and complexity. You can watch real-time progress as your video renders.
What image formats can I upload?
We accept JPG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF files up to 20 MB. For best results, use clear, high-resolution images.

Absolutely. You retain full ownership and copyright of every video you generate on DreamUtopia — use them however you like.

Yes. We use encrypted transfers and do not retain your content after processing. Your uploads and generated videos remain private and under your control.
Can I use DreamUtopia on mobile?
Yes. DreamUtopia works on any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. We also offer dedicated Android and iOS apps for on-the-go creation.
What happens after my 2 free tries?
You can purchase credits to continue generating. The Lite model starts at just 3 credits per generation, with bulk credit discounts available.
