Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026. This clone stores the minimum needed to run accounts and image-to-video jobs.

What we store

  • Email and a PBKDF2 password hash (not the password itself)
  • Credit balance and generation history (prompt, model, status, result URL)
  • Session tokens in Cloudflare KV
  • Uploaded start images in Cloudflare R2, served at unguessable /api/media URLs so the inference provider can fetch them

Providers

Video generation is performed by KIE. Your prompt and source image URL are sent to that API. Result videos are hosted by the provider unless we later copy them into R2.

Cookies and local data

The session token and cached credit count live in your browser’s localStorage. We do not use advertising cookies.

Contact

This is a personal clone project, not the original DreamUtopia product. For data deletion, sign out and email the operator who deployed this Pages project.
